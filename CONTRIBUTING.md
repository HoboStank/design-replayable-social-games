# Contributing

Useful contributions make the skill more specific, testable, or respectful of players.

- Show the problem and the smallest proposed improvement
- Label observations, testimony, and hypotheses separately
- Link primary sources and include a check date for changing platform claims
- Keep the core workflow engine-neutral; put platform examples in source notes
- Use fictional or consented, anonymized examples. Never include private project material, credentials, or player data
- Preserve a compact output: at most three opportunities and one recommended experiment

For wording changes, check relative links and keep `SKILL.md` frontmatter valid. Do not add guaranteed growth claims or present small uncontrolled playtests as causal evidence.

Contributions are welcome under this repository's [MIT license](LICENSE).
