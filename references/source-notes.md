# Optional source notes

These public sources support optional Roblox examples. They are not a complete research review or evidence that the framework causes retention, growth, or revenue improvements. Separate documented features, developer testimony, and design hypotheses. Do not treat platform outcomes as forecasts for another game.

Sources reviewed 2026-10-18:

- [Roblox onboarding guidance](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/onboarding): essential controls, the core loop, early value, visible goals, and investigating drop-off. Adapt to the actual game rather than copying a fixed tutorial.
- [Roblox performance optimization](https://create.roblox.com/docs/performance-optimization): frame rate, memory, and load/join experience. Verify engine-specific practices separately.
- [Roblox developer interview about testing live updates](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/08/optimize-testing-live-updates-roblox-analytics-experimentation-platform): the Adopt Me developer account describes friend-currency incentives producing friend-request spam and retreat to private servers. This is reported experience from one game, not proof that every social incentive fails.
- [Roblox discovery FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq): platform-specific, changeable guidance about recommendation signals. Recheck before quoting current rules. Discovery signals are neither recommended session lengths nor requirements for games distributed elsewhere.

## Boundaries

- Official documentation describes platform guidance; it does not validate a universal design prescription.
- A developer interview is testimony, not independently reproduced experimental evidence.
- Concurrent players, daily active users, lifetime visits, retention, revenue, and profit are different quantities. Do not substitute one for another.
- Rankings, individual server limits, revenue implications, and causal explanations require independent current evidence when a decision depends on them.
- The worked example in this repository is invented to demonstrate the method. It contains no playtest results.
